\begin{frame}{Barren Plateaus — Why Q-LINK Matters}
    \textbf{The problem.} For sufficiently expressive VQAs, gradients vanish exponentially in $n$:
    \[
        \mathrm{Var}_\theta\!\left[\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \theta_k}\right] \le \mathcal{O}(b^{-n}), \quad b > 1
        \qquad \text{(McClean et al.\ 2018)}
    \]
    \textbf{Master formula (Larocca et al.\ 2025).} For $U$ from a 2-design over the DLA,
    with traceless $H = \rho_0 - I/d$ and $V = O - \mathrm{Tr}(O)/d \cdot I$:
    \[
        \mathrm{Var}[\mathcal{L}] \approx \frac{\mathrm{Tr}(H^2)\,\mathrm{Tr}(V^2)}{d^2 - 1}
    \]
    so trainability is controlled by the Hilbert--Schmidt norm of the cost operator $V$
    relative to the Haar volume $d^2$.

    \textbf{Q-LINK's pitch.} Append a single messenger qubit and route information through
    it without intermediate measurements. Compared to measurement-based residuals
    (ResQNets, Kashif \& Al-Kuwari 2024), the messenger pathway preserves unitarity and
    avoids decoherence — the messenger collects phase before each operation layer and
    redistributes it after.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Architecture (Yi \& Bhadani 2026)}
    \begin{center}
        \includegraphics[width=0.72\textwidth]{figure/QLINK.pdf}
    \end{center}
    \small
    \textbf{One layer of Q-LINK} $U_{\text{layer}} = U_{\mathrm{dist}} \, U_{\mathrm{opr}} \, U_{\mathrm{coll}}$, repeated $D$ times.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Collection $U_{\mathrm{coll}}$:} $R_{xx}$ between each data qubit and the messenger; \textbf{Fixed} uses $\theta = \pi/4$, \textbf{Adaptive} makes $\theta$ trainable.
        \item \textbf{Operation $U_{\mathrm{opr}}$:} $R_z R_y R_x$ rotations per data qubit, then a CZ chain across the data register.
        \item \textbf{Distribution $U_{\mathrm{dist}}$:} CNOT fan-out from the messenger to each data qubit.
        \item \textbf{Vanilla:} same $R_zR_yR_x$ + CZ chain on $n$ qubits, no messenger.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Theoretical Prediction}
    \small
    The DLA of Q-LINK on $n_{\mathrm{data}} + 1$ qubits equals the full $\mathfrak{su}(2^{n_{\mathrm{data}}+1})$ (CNOT distribution generates $iZ_m$ from the actual gates; combined with $R_{xx}$ collection it spans every Pauli tensor product). Vanilla on the same total qubit count saturates to the same algebra.

    \textbf{At Haar saturation,} expressibility is identical and the variance ratio reduces to the cost-operator trace ratio:
    \[
        \mathcal{R}(n_{\mathrm{data}}) \;=\; \frac{\mathrm{Tr}(\tilde V^2)}{\mathrm{Tr}(V_{\mathrm{Vanilla}}^2)} \;=\; \frac{2N/n_{\mathrm{data}}}{2N/(n_{\mathrm{data}}+1)} \;=\; \frac{n_{\mathrm{data}}+1}{n_{\mathrm{data}}}, \qquad N = 2^{n_{\mathrm{data}}}
    \]
    Asymptotically $\mathcal{R} \to 1$: the structural Haar advantage is small. Any larger \emph{empirical} advantage in the paper must come from finite-depth Cheeger amplification along the SGD trajectory — not from the ensemble theory.

    \textbf{Parameter-shift rule} gives $\mathrm{Var}[\partial \mathcal{L}/\partial\theta_k] = \tfrac{1}{4}(\mathrm{Var}[\mathcal{L}^+] + \mathrm{Var}[\mathcal{L}^-]) \le \tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{Var}[\mathcal{L}]$ under the 2-design, so the architectural ratio carries from the cost to the gradients.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Part A — Diagnosis: Cost Discrepancy in the Reference Repo}
    \small
    \textbf{Root-cause analysis} (one-pager in repo: \texttt{docs/week3-rca.md}).

    The paper's stated cost (Eq. (2)) is $\mathcal{L} = 1 - \tfrac{1}{n_{\mathrm{data}}}\sum_i \langle Z_i\rangle$,
    observable $\hat O_{\mathrm{paper}} = I - \tfrac{1}{n_{\mathrm{data}}}\sum_i Z_i$ on the data register.

    The reference repository's \texttt{src/qlink/metrics/cost\_function.py} implements
    $1 - \tfrac{1}{n_{\mathrm{data}}}\sum_i P(q_i = |0\rangle)$, i.e.\ exactly \emph{half} the paper's operator.
    For Q-LINK it additionally loops only over the first $2^{n_{\mathrm{data}}}$ entries of a
    $2^{n_{\mathrm{tot}}}$-length probability vector, which projects the first data qubit and
    promotes the messenger into a measured slot:
    \[
        \hat O_{\mathrm{buggy}} \;=\; I \;-\; \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{data}}}\sum_{k=1}^{n_{\mathrm{tot}}-1} P_0^{(q_0)} P_0^{(q_k)}
    \]
    \textbf{Why the targets miss.} The buggy operator's projector on $q_0$ inflates Q-LINK's
    apparent gradient signal by exposing a non-flat slice of the cost landscape; the
    paper's stated cost has no such projector and is flatter, so SGD does not converge in
    1500 steps under the same lr.

    \textbf{Unit-test pinning the diagnosis:} \texttt{test\_buggy\_observable\_matches\_literal\_loop}
    asserts $\hat O_{\mathrm{buggy}}$ produces the same value as the verbatim Python loop on a
    random pure state for $n_{\mathrm{tot}} \in \{3,4,5\}$ (both Vanilla and Q-LINK).
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Part B — Three Extensions (Week-2 hypotheses, signed off)}
    \small
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{E2 — Interaction basis ablation.}
        \emph{Hypothesis:} $R_{yy}$ preserves the DLA isomorphism (full $\mathfrak{su}(2^{n+1})$);
        $R_{zz}$ commutes with the CNOT distribution and fractures the algebra to
        $\mathfrak{su}(2^n)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2^n)$, killing the architectural advantage.
        \emph{Metric:} variance ratio + landscape smoothness vs Vanilla.

        \item \textbf{E3 — Mechanism ablation.}
        \emph{Hypothesis:} Removing $U_{\mathrm{coll}}$ alone or $U_{\mathrm{dist}}$ alone restricts the DLA to
        a direct sum, so both halves are individually necessary. Variance should fall by
        an order of magnitude in each ablated circuit.

        \item \textbf{E5 — Cost-function locality.}
        \emph{Hypothesis:} A normalised $m$-local cost amplifies the trace ratio to
        $\mathcal{R}_m(n) = (n+1)/(n+1-m)$, recovering and exceeding the $m=1$ baseline at finite $n$.
        \emph{Metric:} variance ratio vs theory across $n \in \{3,\ldots,8\}$, $m \in \{1,\ldots,5\}$.
    \end{itemize}

    \textbf{Common methodology.} Same SGD recipe as Part A; per-cell bootstrap CI ($B = 200$
    resamples over seeds); pass/fail/inconclusive verdict per hypothesis.
\end{frame}

    \textbf{Hypothesis (Week 2).} Both halves are individually necessary — ablating
    either collection or distribution should restrict the DLA to a direct sum and kill
    most of the variance gain.

    \textbf{Verdict: \textcolor{miss}{refutes}.}
    \begin{itemize}\small
        \item \textbf{Coll-only $\approx$ Full Q-LINK} at every $n$ tested (ratios $\in [0.92, 1.37]$, CIs overlap throughout). The DLA-closure-via-CNOT argument is therefore \emph{not} a necessary ingredient.
        \item \textbf{Dist-only} matches Full at $n \le 7$ (within $30\%$); $\dagger$ at $n=8$ it spikes to $\sim 30\times$ Full — a stability artifact of unbounded $X$-fanouts without $R_{xx}$ pre-rotation.

The dominant mechanism is the \emph{collection-side} entanglement; the distribution CNOTs are dispensable for variance amplification (though they may matter for landscape shape — to investigate).
    \end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{Limitations \& Honest Notes}
    \small
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{The convergence miss is real.} Under the paper's stated cost the
        SGD setup in the paper does not converge in 1500 iters at any $n$ in my hands.
        The reference repo uses a different operator (Slide 11). I report \emph{both};
        the body of the slides uses the paper's stated cost as the assignment requires.

        \item \textbf{Expressibility metric degenerates at large $n$.} With $d = 2^n$,
        the Haar fidelity PDF concentrates in the first histogram bin; KL $\to 0$ is a
        property of the discretisation, not of the circuit. A frame-potential metric
        would be more discriminating; out of scope for one month.

        \item \textbf{No GPU.} Runs are CPU-only on a Mac M-series; the paper used an
        RTX 4090. Wall-clock numbers in the report are not directly comparable.

        \item \textbf{TC vs Qulacs sign convention.} Both backends agree on
        \emph{probabilities} but Qulacs uses $e^{+i\theta/2}$ while TC uses $e^{-i\theta/2}$.
        Optimizer negates at the boundary so trajectories live in TC-convention
        parameter space; full equivalence test ships with the repo.

        \item \textbf{Bootstrap CIs in Part B} are over 5 seeds — wide. Better
        uncertainty quantification would need $\ge 20$ seeds, dropped to keep within the
        one-month budget.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
